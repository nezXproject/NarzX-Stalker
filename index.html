How to reduce what you find
  • Documents and shared files: delete or restrict the source file, then request removal from Google's "Remove outdated content" and "Results about you" tools.
  • Calendars: make them private in your calendar's sharing settings; revoke public ICS links.
  • Data brokers: use each site's opt-out form, then re-scan in a few weeks.
  • Profiles: tighten visibility, remove phone/email fields, and use separate aliases for public accounts.
  • Pastes and repositories: ask the host to take it down, and rotate anything sensitive that was exposed.
